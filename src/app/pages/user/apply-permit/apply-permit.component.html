Why Do You Need the Map?

The map is an essential feature of this system as it helps applicants and administrators visualize the location of the business. It ensures that:

  • Businesses are within the jurisdiction of the local government unit.
  • Easy identification of business zones and compliance with zoning regulations.
  • Facilitation of inspections by local authorities.
  • Improved transparency and accountability for both applicants and government officials.

How to Use the Map

  1. Search for your business location using the map.
  2. Drag the pin on your business location to pin it accurately on the map.
  3. Verify the address displayed on the map matches your business address.
  4. Submit the pinned location as part of your application.

Ensure that your business location is accurate to avoid delays in approval or issues during inspections.
