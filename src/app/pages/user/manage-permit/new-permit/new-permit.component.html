Why Do You Need the Map?

The map is an important feature of this system as it helps both applicants and administrators visualize the exact location of a business. It ensures that:

  • Businesses are located within the jurisdiction of the local government unit.
  • Business zones are easily identified for compliance with zoning regulations.
  • Local authorities can facilitate inspections more efficiently.
  • Transparency and accountability are improved for both applicants and officials.

How to Use the Map

  1. Move or drag the map background until your business location is positioned at the center pin.
  2. Zoom in or out if necessary for more precise positioning.
  3. Ensure that the central pin matches your actual business address.
  4. Save or confirm the pinned location to include it in your application.

Make sure the pin is centered on your exact business location to avoid delays in approval or inspection issues.
