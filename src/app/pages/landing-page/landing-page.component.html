Through the Integrated Business Permits and Licensing System (iBPLS), you may now secure your Business Permit in just 3 steps:

  1. Step 1: Application and Verification - Fill out and submit your application form for verification.
  2. Step 2: Assessment and Payment - Pay the required fees after assessment of your application.
  3. Step 3: Releasing - Once approved, your business permit will be released for you to collect.

Application of Business Permit

Who must avail of the service?

All constituents who plan to operate a new business or to continue operating an existing business are required to secure Mayor's Permit.

APPLYING FOR BUSINESS REGISTRATION

Requirements for New Business

  • Unified Application Form
  • Barangay Clearance
  • BFP-Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC)
  • Occupancy Permit Building Official
  • Zoning Clearance
  • Environmental Compliance Certificate
  • Sanitary Permit

Requirements for Renewal of Business Permits

  • Unified Application Form
  • Barangay Clearance
  • Old Business Permit
