Admin Reminder:

This panel displays the complete details of the applicant’s submitted business permit application. Review all information carefully before performing any action on behalf of the concerned department.

  • All submitted details, documents, and uploaded files appear exactly as provided by the applicant.
  • Validate business information, owner details, and attached documentary requirements.
  • Check the accuracy, clarity, and completeness of the uploaded requirements before proceeding.
  • Ensure all mandatory files, forms, and signatures (if required) are properly submitted.
  • Missing or questionable information may require coordination with the applicant for clarification.
  • Actions taken on this application (Approve, Review, Decline) will be tagged under the corresponding department.
  • No direct edits can be made to the applicant’s submission from this screen.
  • Once approved by the required departments, the application will continue to permit processing.
