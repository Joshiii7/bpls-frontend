Requirements & Guidelines

To apply for a new business permit or renew an existing one, please prepare the following documents and follow these steps.

Business Registration Form

Fully filled-out BPLS registration form with all business details.

Proof of Identity

Valid ID of the business owner or authorized representative.

Barangay Clearance

Latest barangay clearance from the business location.

DTI / SEC Registration

Certificate of business name registration or corporation registration.

Occupancy / Zoning Permit

Proof that the business location complies with local zoning and occupancy regulations.

Other Supporting Documents

Any additional documents required depending on the type of business (e.g., health clearance, fire safety inspection).

Application Guidelines

  1. Prepare all required documents listed above before starting the application.
  2. Ensure that all personal and business information is accurate and complete.
  3. Submit the application online through the BPLS portal or at the municipal office if needed.
  4. Pay the corresponding fees online or at the designated office.
  5. Wait for verification and approval notifications via email or SMS.
  6. Download or print your business permit once approved.
