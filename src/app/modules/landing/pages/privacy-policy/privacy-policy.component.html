This Privacy Policy applies to the Business Permit and Licensing System (BPLS), developed as an academic project. It is not currently connected to a live government office, and information submitted through it is not processed by an actual permitting authority.

What Happens to Information You Enter

Forms on this system, including registration, sign in, and the contact form, are handled within your browser session. In this deployment, nothing you type is sent to an external server, stored permanently, or shared with anyone.

Local Session Data

Some features, like signing in or submitting a sample application, store information temporarily in your browser's local storage so the system behaves the way it would in a live environment. You can clear this at any time using the reset option, or by clearing your browser's site data.

Cookies and Tracking

This system doesn't use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

Questions

If you have questions about how this system handles your information, you're welcome to reach out through the contact page.
